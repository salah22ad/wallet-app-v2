💎 Track My Account Feature Guide

Instant financial transparency letting you and your customers view live balances anytime

🏪 Why is this feature important?

  • Reduce daily calls and messages asking about balances
  • Organize accounts clearly and transparently
  • Save time and effort for both merchants and customers
  • Allow customers to review their own statements anytime

🛡️ Security and Verification

To protect privacy and prevent unauthorized access, the app uses two-factor verification via an SMS verification code to confirm phone ownership. You can deactivate this feature anytime from settings.

📱 Feature Usage Terms

  • Both parties must activate the Track My Account feature.
  • No one can view accounts without activating the feature and verifying their phone number.
  • You can disable the feature at any time from app settings.

🔔 Reminder: Never share your SMS verification code with anyone.

🚀 Activation Steps

Open My Smart Wallet app
Tap Track My Accounts from the bottom bar
Enter your business name and valid phone number
Use the same number when managing customer accounts

🗣️ How to inform your customers?

"To check your account balance anytime, activate the Track My Account feature in My Smart Wallet app and view your statements without calls or waiting."
